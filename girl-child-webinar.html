The Conversation

Why this matters.

Every girl deserves the freedom to learn, grow, dream and determine the direction of her own future.

Yet child marriage and other barriers continue to limit the opportunities available to millions of girls. Ending these barriers requires more than awareness — it requires investment, advocacy, education and action.

Through this webinar, AIFSA Worldwide brings together young people, advocates, students and interested participants for a meaningful conversation about protecting girls' rights and building a future where every girl can thrive.
